  • Voice and chat. Jarvis answers Telegram messages or a “Hey Jarvis” wake word, with offline speech-to-text and spoken replies.
  • Tool-using agent. Reads Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Sheets with read-only access, and runs PC actions.
  • Model fallback. Uses Gemini first and falls back to a local Ollama model when it is unreachable or rate-limited.
  • Locked-down PC control. A localhost-only server with a fixed action whitelist, no shell access, separate API keys and an owner-only allowlist.
  • Job posting to draft. Extracts the details, scores fit against my resume, flags scam risks and drafts an application that follows the posting’s own instructions.
  • Human in the loop. Two-stage approval means nothing is drafted or emailed without a button press, and duplicate applications are blocked.

Hobby project spotlight: LipiRx

A personal project I built for learning and as a hobby, to explore Kotlin Multiplatform and AI integration. It is designed for retail pharmacies in Bangladesh: point the camera at a doctor’s prescription and it fills in the details.

Personal projectKotlin MultiplatformCompose MultiplatformMulti-agent AIVision AIGeminiGroqOpenAIOllama
View on GitHub
  • Multi-agent pipeline. A manager agent coordinates handwriting OCR, medical entity parsing, generic and substitute lookup, and a patient safety audit.
  • Brand names. Finds each medicine written on the prescription.
  • Generic compositions. Matches each brand to its active ingredient.
  • Local dosage schedules. Understands the dosing notation used locally.
  • Tablet quantities. Calculates how many tablets to hand over.
  • Similar brands. Suggests alternative brands for each medicine.
  • Needs review flags. Marks unclear items so the pharmacist confirms before dispensing.
  • Zoom and pan. Inspect the original photo next to the result.
  • Your choice of AI provider. Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, including a local Ollama server or a private proxy.
1. ScanTake a photo of the handwritten prescription.
2. ReadA pipeline of AI agents reads the handwriting, matches brands and audits the result.
3. ReviewCheck brands, generics, dosage schedules and tablet counts.

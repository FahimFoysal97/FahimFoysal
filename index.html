LipiRx reads handwritten prescriptions for pharmacies.

A Kotlin Multiplatform app for retail pharmacies in Bangladesh. Point the camera at a doctor’s prescription and it fills in the details for you.

Kotlin MultiplatformCompose MultiplatformVision AIGeminiGroqOpenAIOllama
View on GitHub
  • Brand names. Finds each medicine written on the prescription.
  • Generic compositions. Matches each brand to its active ingredient.
  • Local dosage schedules. Understands the dosing notation used locally.
  • Tablet quantities. Calculates how many tablets to hand over.
  • Similar brands. Suggests alternative brands for each medicine.
  • Needs review flags. Marks unclear items so the pharmacist confirms before dispensing.
  • Zoom and pan. Inspect the original photo next to the result.
  • Your choice of AI provider. Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, including a local Ollama server or a private proxy.
1. ScanTake a photo of the handwritten prescription.
2. ReadVision AI reads it with the provider you chose.
3. ReviewCheck brands, generics, dosage schedules and tablet counts.
